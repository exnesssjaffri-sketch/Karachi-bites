Generational Craft

Seventy Years of Hand-Ground Spices, Charcoal Coals, and Culinary Discipline.

When our founders set up their first iron tawa and copper cauldron on Burns Road in 1952, Karachi was rapidly transforming into the culinary heartbeat of the subcontinent. What never changed was the uncompromising reverence for time.

We do not use commercial flavor enhancers, frozen proteins, or high-pressure speed shortcuts. Our nihari simmers through the dark hours under clay lids; our seekh kebabs are shaped onto iron skewers by master hands; and our biryani pots are sealed with wheat dough to capture every ounce of fragrant steam.

No Shortcuts
