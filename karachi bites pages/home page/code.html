The Burns Road Lineage

Decades of Slow Smoke,
Secret Masalas & Pure Dedication.

In the narrow, bustling arteries of Burns Road, time slows down around massive copper cauldrons. For over 70 years, our spice formulations have been hand-pounded using mortar and pestle, balancing star anise, black cumin, mace, and Kashmiri chilies.

We don't use commercial shortcuts or synthetic tenderizers. Every batch of nihari is simmered throughout the quiet hours of the night so the collagen naturally thickens the gravy. When you order from Karachi Bites, you receive recipes preserved through generations.
